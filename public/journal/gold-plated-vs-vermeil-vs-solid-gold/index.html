    Gold-plated vs vermeil vs solid gold: what's the difference?

    All four look like gold. The difference is how much gold there is, and what sits underneath.

    Solid gold

    Gold is measured in karats out of 24. 24K is about 99.9% gold, 22K about 91.6%, 21K about 87.5% and 18K is 75%. Higher karats are richer in colour and softer. Solid gold is the most durable and the most expensive.

    Gold-filled

    A thick layer of gold is bonded to a base metal under heat and pressure. It lasts much longer than plating and costs less than solid gold.

    Gold vermeil

    Gold over sterling silver, with a thicker layer than standard plating. In the US, the term is defined as sterling silver coated with gold of at least 10 karats and at least 2.5 microns thick.

    Gold-plated

    A thin layer of real gold applied to a base metal by electroplating. It gives the warm look of gold at an accessible price, which makes it ideal for trend pieces, layering and gifts. With care, plating keeps its shine; with heavy wear it can soften over time.
